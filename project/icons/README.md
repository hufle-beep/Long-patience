# Icons

The game embeds 32 icons from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) (source: github.com/game-icons/icons) in `index.html` (`GI`, UI script). They are licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) by their authors: Lorc, Delapouite and Skoll.

Changes: the black background square removed, the white fill replaced by the text colour (`currentColor`), stored as one path each. No endorsement by the authors or game-icons.net is implied. The in-game credit is in Settings > Credits ("Icons made by Lorc, Delapouite and Skoll. Available on game-icons.net", with the licence link), as the authors ask; keep it there and in any store listing.

When adding an icon: take one with a single path (no `<g>` or `transform`), ideally under ~2 KB, add its author here and to the Credits text if new.
